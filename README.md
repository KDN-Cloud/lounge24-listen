# 📻 Lounge24 Radio

A 24/7 internet radio station from Tone Studio in Las Vegas, NV. No algorithms, no ads — just music. AutoDJ keeps the rotation moving between live DJ sets and guest broadcasts.

## 🎧 Listen

**[Open the Lounge24 web player](https://radio.aklein.studio/public/lounge24_radio)** — no account or sign-up required. Press play to tune in.

| Listen with | Link |
| --- | --- |
| Your browser | [Web player](https://radio.aklein.studio/public/lounge24_radio) |
| VLC, Audacious, or another network audio player | [Direct MP3 stream](https://radio.aklein.studio/listen/lounge24_radio/radio.mp3) |
| A player that supports M3U playlists | [Download M3U](https://radio.aklein.studio/public/lounge24_radio/playlist.m3u) |
| A player that supports PLS playlists | [Download PLS](https://radio.aklein.studio/public/lounge24_radio/playlist.pls) |

For an external player, open the playlist or paste the direct MP3 URL into its network-stream option. If your browser downloads a playlist instead of playing it, open that file in your audio player.

Prefer to listen while browsing the studio site? Use the player at [aklein.studio](https://www.aklein.studio/). On desktop, **OPEN ↗** launches a separate player window that you can keep open as you browse.

## 🎼 What We Play

The rotation pulls from a wide range of sounds:

- **House / Deep House**
- **Jungle / Drum & Bass**
- **Trip-Hop**
- **Trance / Psytrance**
- **Dubstep**
- **Jazz**
- **Reggae / Dub**
- **Classical**
- **Metal / Rock**
- **Lounge / Chill**
- **Recorded mixes** — sets from the archive, spanning years of digging

It's a mixed bag on purpose. The vibe shifts throughout the day.

## Playback Notes

- Browsers may require a click or tap before audio starts; press **PLAY** and check the player and device volume.
- On the studio site, **LIVE** means the station is broadcasting, including AutoDJ. It does not necessarily mean a DJ is currently on the decks.
- If playback stops, pause and play again to reconnect. You can also try the web player or direct MP3 stream above.
- For past sessions rather than the current broadcast, visit the [mix archive](https://www.aklein.studio/mixes).
